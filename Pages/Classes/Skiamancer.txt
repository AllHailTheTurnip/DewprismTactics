Title: Skiamancer
Subtitle: Soul Binder
Element: Darkness
Description: Skiamancers in their past lifes were powerful warlocks who were driven to
unraveling all the mysteries of life and death. Yet in having been granted their wish,
they were reborn from the beginning as husks of their former selves. Now living a vacuous
existence, they drawn strength by stealing the souls of slain foes.

ABILITIES

Doctrine: Steal Shadow
Will-Support-Trigger
Trigger: You are in the same space as a dead enemy.
Use: (1/turn)
Range: 0sp
Effect: Add a Shadow token to yourself and destroy the enemy's body.
Choose one: Mode A, Mode B, or Mode C.
Mode A: Up to (3) times, increase increase your Health, Mana, Stamina, and/or Focus by 5 points.
Mode B: Up to (2) times, increease your MlePwr, MgcPwr, or SklPwr by 1 point.
Mode C: Multiply the rank of a non-Doctrine ability by 1.5.

A1: Shade Servant
Will-Support-Action
Use: 2AP+8mna
Range: 1sp
Can only use on dead combatant.
Accuracy: Magic
Effect: Create a Shade Servant under your control with the same Stats, Abilities and memories as the dead combatant.
Its Element will be Darkness and it'll take its turn after yours.
It has no health, but for every (5) damage it takes, your mana pool subtracts 4 points.
You may dismiss the Shade Servant at any point, where it'll return to your space.

A2: Cast Shadow
Will-Support-Action
Use: 1AP+8mna
Effect: Separate your shadow from yourself as an individual entity.
Your shadow has 2AP to spend once per turn.
Similar to a shadow, it can climb up and down sheer surfaces as if they were flat.
Your shadow shares its senses with you.
If it takes damage, the spell is broken and the shadow fades away.

A3: Shadow Step
Will-Movement-Action
Use: 1AP+8mna
Range: (5)sp
Effect: While in a shaded spot, you can transport to another shaded spot on the battlefield.

A4: Blind
Will-Offense-Action
Use: 2AP+16mna
Range: 3sp
Accuracy: Magic
Effect: Target takes the Blind (2) status effect.
Blind (X): Target's Accuracy checks take -5 and Movement actions have a Luck10 roll to head into the correct space. The effect lasts for X turns.

A5: Second Strike
Will-Defense-Reaction
Trigger: Your health has dropped below 0 and you aren't dead.
Use: (1/battle)+8sta.
Effect: Make a free attack using a nat-20 on the Accuracy roll. Any damage inflicted is regained as Health.

A6: Curse of Stagnation
Will-Offense-Action

Range: 3ps AoE
Evade: Magic
Effect: Upon failure to resist, target is afflicted with Stagnation (5).
