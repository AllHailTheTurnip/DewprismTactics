Title: Gorotan
Subtitle: Electrically Charged Hunter
Element: Electric
Description: The first Gorotans were disciples of the feline god Goro, a massive feline
who first created lightning and sent Omnitheon to the abyss in the First of Days. Their
ancient order has existed since the first millennia, and though now it is old and whithered,
these thunderous monks continue live the legend.

Will-Offensive-Action
Use: 2AP
Range: 3sp+{TL/4}
Accuracy: Skill
Damage: Deal electric (8){MgcPwr} to the target.
