Classes

Below are the playable classes of Dewprism Tactics, adapted from the video game. A class is a title, an element and a set of abilities. Health, Stamina, Mana, Focus, cores and Initiative belong to the character, not the class.

  • Doctrine: the class's flagship ability. The Doctrine in a character's Doctrine slot is their primary class; abilities from other classes can be equipped freely.
  • Class levels: every class in a battle, on either side, earns each combatant 10 class XP per combatant who has it as their primary class (100 XP per class level). Level 1 teaches the Doctrine, levels 2–10 one of the nine standard abilities each, and the three Master abilities come at the next level after all nine are learned (normally 11), then at 20 and 30.
  • Scale: ability ranks run VL 4, L 6, M 8, H 12, VH 16, SH 24. Energy costs are 4 to 16 (Master abilities 12 to 48), and every combatant gets 4 AP a turn; costs above that are charged over several turns.
